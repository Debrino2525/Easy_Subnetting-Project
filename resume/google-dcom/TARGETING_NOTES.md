# Targeting Notes — Google Data Center Operations Manager

## Minimum qualifications mapping

| Google requirement | How this resume addresses it |
|---|---|
| Bachelor's in a technical field **or equivalent practical experience** | Associate Degree (IT) + BSc IT in progress + 9+ years technical experience framed as equivalent practical experience |
| 8 years in computing infrastructure, networking, OS, or hardware | Bank of Ghana data center NOC; GRIDCo/VRA hardware & OS support; Helios infrastructure audits; Sophies infrastructure assessments |
| 5 years managing technical, vendor, contract, or delivery teams | Emphasizes **vendor/contractor coordination**, contract verification (Helios), multi-country delivery (Sophies), and escalation leadership—without inventing a formal people-manager title |
| Non-standard hours / shift schedules | Explicitly stated as willing |
| Travel 50% | Explicitly stated as available; Tel Aviv relocation willingness added |

## Responsibilities mapping

- **Lead / coach people** → incident leadership, escalation coordination, training/coaching 60+ staff (not claimed as direct-report people management)
- **Security & operational procedures + trend analysis** → NOC/SIEM monitoring, procedure reinforcement, executive/operational reporting
- **Repair / preventative maintenance mindset** → hardware support, patching, facility monitoring, infrastructure troubleshooting
- **EHS / compliance collaboration** → EPA/power work, physical security, ISO 27001, critical-infrastructure training

If you have **any** formal team leadership (direct reports, shift lead, contractor crew size, or vendor SLAs owned), share those numbers—adding them would strengthen the “5 years managing teams” minimum qualification significantly.
