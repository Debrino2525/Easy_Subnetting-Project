# What Changed in This Resume Rewrite

## Structural improvements

1. **Clear professional positioning** — Added a cybersecurity-focused tagline (SOC · Penetration Testing · Governance & Compliance) so recruiters see the target role in under 3 seconds.
2. **Stronger summary** — Rewrote the summary around outcomes and domain coverage (finance, telecom, critical infrastructure) instead of a flat skills list.
3. **Categorized competencies** — Replaced a flat bullet list (including vague items like “Excellent Analytical Skills”) with four scannable groups: Security Operations, Offensive Security, Governance & Risk, and Infrastructure.
4. **Consolidated overlapping roles** — Merged the two Sophies Group penetration-testing entries (Jul 2019–Nov 2022 and Feb 2020–Sep 2020) into one coherent role with the strongest bullets from both.
5. **Cleaner education & training** — Separated degree programs from short courses; highlighted high-signal DoD / DHS / ISO training without burying certifications.
6. **Removed outdated / noisy items** — Dropped “References upon request,” duplicate language entries (Fanti/Twi listed twice), and the tracking footer ID.

## Content & language improvements

- Fixed tense and grammar consistency across bullets.
- Kept measurable impact where the original had it (e.g., 500+ tower sites, 60+ staff trained).
- Made bullets more action-oriented and easier to scan for ATS and hiring managers.
- Emphasized SIEM, incident response, red teaming, and ISO 27001 — aligned with the strongest parts of the profile.
